      What you need, and where to find it

      1. Keys live in one file: .env, in the Hive-Mind folder. Open it in the terminal with nano .env. Never paste a key in chat, and never type one on this page.
      2. Meta model: get your key at dev.meta.ai, under API Keys. Check Billing there too, so you know your free credits. Put the key in .env as META_API_KEY.
      3. Ollama (on your own computer): no key needed. Use the model name shown by ollama list.
      4. OpenRouter: get your key from your OpenRouter account page, and use the exact provider/model name from your account.
      5. After editing .env, start a fresh terminal so the new settings are picked up.
      6. Full steps for every app and model are in docs/INTEGRATIONS.md, in the Hive-Mind folder.

      Plain-language guide

      Every button and box on this page has a short, plain-words tip. Hold your pointer over a button or box for half a second to read its tip. You can turn tips off in Regular Settings.

      • A memory is one thing you asked Hive-Mind to remember, like "The shop opens at 9am."
      • The picture shows your memories as dots and shapes. Lines join memories that share a word or idea. Every dot is also written out as words below the picture, so you never have to rely on colour alone.
      • A snapshot is a backup copy of all your memories. It is made on the schedule you choose, and kept where you choose. Making a backup copy never deletes anything.
      • The token meter counts how much work the app has done. Your token budget is the most work it may do for one answer. Bigger answers use more tokens.
      • The micro-bot is a small helper. Simple mode writes you a short morning summary. Pro mode watches for changes and only acts within the spending limit you set.
      • The Accessibility Suite only changes how the page looks and reads: colours, text size, spacing, and movement. It never deletes or changes your memories.
      • Growth stage is how grown your memory sky is. A Nebula becomes a Protostar, then a Star, then a Radiant Sun as you add memories and check facts. The stage is always written out in words next to the picture.
      • Origins say who made each memory — a model, an app, or you. Each origin has its own ring colour and its own ring pattern (solid, dashed, dotted, or double), listed in the Origins list under the picture with a count, so you can tell them apart even in grayscale. You can change the colours in Regular Settings.
      • EXP is points for keeping memories healthy: checked facts, settled mix-ups, and backups. EXP never costs money and never buys anything.
      • Integrations shows which apps and models are connected to Hive-Mind, and gives you the setup text to copy. Keys are never typed on this page: they live in the .env file, edited with nano in the terminal.
      • Demo play: press Play demo above the picture to watch the page walk through the demo steps by itself — the growth stage, EXP, origins, the graph view, grayscale, and Integrations. Press Play again to pause, Escape to stop. Your colours, profile, and graph view come back exactly as they were when it stops or finishes.
      • If anything ever looks wrong, press Reset accessibility settings to go back to the starting look.
